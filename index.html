FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find yours? Our team replies within a day.

How often do I change the filter?

About every 6 months. The Blueair app tells you when it's time.

How loud is it?

On sleep mode it runs at 24 dB. On full speed it's about 52 dB, like a quiet conversation.

What size room does it cover?

Mini covers 215 sq ft, Home covers 540 sq ft and Max covers 900 sq ft.

How much power does it use?

Home uses about 30 watts on medium. That's close to a small lamp.

Can I return it?

Yes. You get 30 days to try it at home, and returns are free.

Ready for fresher air?
